      How it works

      1. Open on your e-reader: Open Balkan BookDrop on your Kobo, Kindle, or Tolino to see a 4-character key and QR code.
      2. Enter key & pick books: Scan the QR code with your phone or type the key, then drop ebook files (or extract from ZIP, RAR, 7Z, or TAR archives) and press Upload and send.
      3. Download immediately: Download links appear on your e-reader screen within seconds.

      You can also search the BalkanDownload catalog directly from this page to find books. Files are stored temporarily on the server and deleted automatically a few minutes after you close the e-reader browser or generate a new key.
